The Invitation

With the blessings of God and the joy of two families coming together, Mr. Varughese Mathew & Mrs. Sherly Varughese and Mr. Koshy Skaria & Mrs. Sissy Koshy cordially invite you to celebrate the wedding of their children Asish Varghese with Rachel Koshy.
